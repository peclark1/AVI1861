# J1 plug-in hardware

J1 is intentionally excluded from PCBWay assembly.

Recommended hardware: **two** 12-position single-row Mill-Max Series 350 round-pin headers:

- Manufacturer part: `350-10-112-00-006000`
- DigiKey value-added alias/orderable listing: `ED6864-12-ND`
- Pitch: 0.100 in / 2.54 mm
- Contact shape: circular
- Mating length: 0.142 in / 3.61 mm
- PCB post length: 0.110 in / 2.79 mm
- Gold finish
- Quantity per AVI1861: 2 strips

Install the two strips in the two 12-pin rows of the CDP1861-compatible 24-pin footprint after PCBWay SMT assembly. Use a spare quality 24-pin 0.6-inch DIP socket as an alignment fixture while soldering so the rows remain parallel and at the correct 0.600-inch spacing.

The PCB drill is 0.8 mm at J1, which is appropriate for the through-hole round-pin header footprint. Final mechanical fit should still be checked on the first assembled module before installing the remaining modules.
